## Why this shape

- **One recommendation, not options** — SMEDW needs a plan to re-plan around, not a decision
  to make on our behalf.
- **The estimate carries its own caveat** (the breaking assumption) so it reads as honest
  sizing, not a promise.
- **The next action is the last line** — it's what turns "here's an assessment" into
  something SMEDW can actually act on Monday.
- **The offer to walk through it live** is genuine, not filler — a scope this size lands
  better with a chance for questions than as a wall of text.
