## Why this shape

- **The date is the first sentence.** That's the entire point of the message.
- **Scope + plan, not migration** is stated explicitly so the commitment can't be misheard
  as "migrated by Sept 9" — that misread would restart the stall in two weeks.
- **The last line hands Ryan a decision**, which makes the date collaborative instead of
  unilateral, without weakening it.
